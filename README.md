#  My Favorite Movie: *Spider-Man*

##  Overview
*Spider-Man* is a **superhero movie** based on the Marvel Comics character created by **Stan Lee** and **Steve Ditko**.  
It tells the story of **Peter Parker**, an ordinary teenager who gains superpowers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

---

##  Main Characters
- **Peter Parker / Spider-Man**  
  The main hero who learns that *“with great power comes great responsibility.”*  
- **Mary Jane Watson** 
  Peter’s love interest and emotional anchor.
